## Purpose And Scope

This contract defines how an implementation, product or component is evaluated
against a versioned Hiaka specification.

Conformance is evidence-based. A declaration of alignment without inspectable
evidence is not a conformance claim.

## Normative Requirements

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| `CONF-001` | A conformance claim **MUST** identify the specification version and primary profile evaluated. |
| `CONF-002` | Every applicable `MUST` and `MUST NOT` requirement **MUST** have inspectable evidence. |
| `CONF-003` | Every unmet `SHOULD` or `SHOULD NOT` requirement **MUST** include a documented exception and rationale. |
| `CONF-004` | Non-applicable requirements **MUST** include a scope-based justification. |
| `CONF-005` | Evidence **MUST** identify its type, location, owner and review date. |
| `CONF-006` | Conformance reports **MUST** preserve requirement identifiers exactly. |
| `CONF-007` | A result **MUST** be classified as conformant, partially conformant or non-conformant. |
| `CONF-008` | Any unmet applicable mandatory requirement **MUST** prevent a conformant result. |
| `CONF-009` | Conformance reports **MUST** distinguish missing evidence from evidence of non-conformance. |
| `CONF-010` | Conformance evidence **SHOULD** be reproducible or independently reviewable. |

## Evidence Matrix

Use one row for each applicable or explicitly non-applicable requirement:

| Requirement | Applicability | Evidence type | Evidence location | Result | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| API-002 | Applicable | OpenAPI document | `openapi.yaml` | Pass | Version validated in CI |

Accepted evidence types include contracts, schemas, automated tests, policy
definitions, accessibility reviews, localization reviews and documented
architectural decisions.

## Result Classification

### Conformant

All applicable mandatory requirements pass with inspectable evidence. Any
exception to a recommended requirement is documented and accepted in the
report.

### Partially Conformant

The evaluation remains incomplete because required evidence is missing, but no
mandatory violation has been demonstrated. The report must name every gap and
must not present the result as conformant.

### Non-Conformant

Evidence demonstrates violation of one or more applicable mandatory
requirements.

## Missing Evidence Versus Failure

Missing evidence means the reviewer cannot verify an obligation. Evidence of
non-conformance means the reviewer can verify that the obligation is not met.
Both prevent a conformant result, but they require different remediation.

## Expected Evidence

The conformance report itself must include:

* specification identity and version;
* primary profile;
* evaluation scope;
* completed evidence matrix;
* exceptions and non-applicability rationales;
* final classification;
* evaluator, owner and review date.

## Conformant And Non-Conformant Claims

Conformant claim:

```text
Public Consultation API draft 0.1 is conformant with API-001 through API-010.
The evidence matrix links each requirement to OpenAPI and contract tests.
```

Non-conformant claim:

```text
This product follows Hiaka best practices.
```

The second statement identifies no specification version, profile,
requirements or evidence.

## Non-Goals

This contract does not create certification authority, automated scoring,
procurement policy or stable-status governance.

## Dependencies

Conformance evaluates the applicable framework profile and cross-cutting
contracts. It does not replace their domain-specific requirements.
