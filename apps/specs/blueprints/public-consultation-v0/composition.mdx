Public Consultation v0 composes reusable Civic Stack Model components. The
Blueprint defines their roles and dependencies without absorbing their business
rules.

## Required Civic Modules

### Participation

Participation owns:

* consultation identity and metadata;
* lifecycle and stage configuration;
* contribution period;
* territorial scope;
* eligibility policy reference;
* process publication and externally visible status.

### Contribution

Contribution owns:

* contribution drafts and submissions;
* contribution publication;
* moderation state and reason;
* attachment and geographic references where enabled;
* institutional responses linked to contributions.

### Accountability

Accountability owns:

* commitments;
* responsible organizations;
* milestones;
* progress updates;
* evidence;
* completion state and history.

## Optional Civic Modules

Deliberation is optional. It is required only when the consultation exposes a
public `DELIBERATE` stage with discussion capabilities.

Decision is optional. It is required only when the consultation adds a formal
selection, prioritization or decision mechanism.

Neither Module blocks the Public Consultation v0 critical path.

## Required Civic Design System Patterns

### Inform

Inform makes institutional ownership, status, dates, scope, results and next
steps understandable.

### Consult

Consult frames the public question, eligible audience, contribution period and
expected institutional outcome.

### Contribute

Contribute guides submission, validation, publication state, moderation state
and participant follow-up.

## Provisional Pattern Mappings

Decide is provisionally mapped to institutional response and rationale.

Account is provisionally mapped to commitments, progress and public evidence.

These mappings guide experience design but do not support complete Civic Design
System conformance until dedicated Decide and Account pattern specs exist.

## Shared Digital Services

| Service | Classification | Trigger | Consumer | Interface boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Authentication | Required | Participant or institutional action | Participation and application boundary | Authentication port |
| Identity or verification | Conditional | Eligibility policy requires verified attributes | Participation | Identity or verification port |
| Territory registry | Conditional | Consultation or eligibility is territorial | Participation | Territory port |
| Document storage | Conditional | Official documents, attachments or evidence are enabled | Participation, Contribution, Accountability | Document port |
| Notifications | Optional | Process configuration enables proactive updates | All required Modules | Notification port |
| Audit infrastructure | Required | Sensitive institutional or moderation action occurs | All required Modules | Audit port |

No provider implementation is mandatory. Adapters translate provider behavior
into the published capability contract.

## Composition Boundary

The Blueprint can require a capability and constrain its role in the process.
It does not define Module DTOs, persistence models or internal invariants.

Future Module specs remain the source of truth for commands, queries, events,
authorization policies and domain behavior.

## Normative Requirements

### BP-PC-011

Public Consultation v0 **MUST** compose Participation, Contribution and
Accountability as required Civic Modules.

**Responsible component:** Blueprint composition.

**Related contracts:** `SPEC-007`, `INT-005`.

**Expected evidence:** component manifest and end-to-end composition tests.

### BP-PC-012

The Blueprint **MUST NOT** duplicate or take ownership of business rules owned
by its required or optional Civic Modules.

**Responsible component:** Blueprint specification.

**Related contracts:** `SPEC-007`, `API-003`.

**Expected evidence:** ownership matrix and review against Module contracts.

### BP-PC-013

The Blueprint **MUST** apply Inform, Consult and Contribute as required Civic
Design System patterns.

**Responsible component:** Blueprint experience composition.

**Related contracts:** `SPEC-008`, `CONF-001`.

**Expected evidence:** Design System mapping and experience conformance review.

### BP-PC-014

Decide and Account mappings **MUST** be disclosed as provisional until their
dedicated pattern specifications exist.

**Responsible component:** Blueprint experience composition.

**Related contracts:** `CONF-003`, `CONF-009`.

**Expected evidence:** disclosed conformance gaps and mapping references.

### BP-PC-015

Every Shared Digital Service dependency **MUST** be classified as required,
conditional or optional and consumed through an explicit interface.

**Responsible components:** consuming Modules and infrastructure adapters.

**Related contracts:** `INT-003`, `INT-004`, `INT-005`.

**Expected evidence:** dependency matrix, port definitions and adapter contract
tests.
