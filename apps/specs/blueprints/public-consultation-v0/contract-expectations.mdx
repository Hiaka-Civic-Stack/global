producer behavior.

## Authorization Matrix

| Actor | Allowed actions | Required scope | Denial expectation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public Visitor | Read published civic information | Published resources | Unpublished or restricted resources remain inaccessible |
| Participant | Create and submit own contribution | Open process, eligibility, territory and ownership | Explicit non-sensitive denial |
| Institution Manager | Configure, publish, close, respond and manage commitments | Organization, process and territory | Default deny outside accountable scope |
| Moderator | Review, restrict or approve contributions | Enabled role, process and moderation policy | No moderation permission when role or policy is absent |
| Facilitator | Explicit support actions | Granted process capability | No implicit moderation or institutional permission |

Authorization is enforced at the application or domain boundary, never only in
the frontend.

## Mandatory Audit Actions

Audit evidence is required for:

* consultation publication and closure;
* moderation decisions;
* institutional responses;
* outcome summary publication;
* commitment creation and material modification;
* commitment progress, evidence and completion;
* eligibility or moderation policy changes.

Audit records capture accountable actor, action, civic object, timestamp,
state transition, reason and provenance where relevant.

## Normative Requirements

### BP-PC-026

The Blueprint specification **MUST** identify expected API actions and map each
action to its responsible Module and applicable cross-cutting requirements.

**Responsible component:** Blueprint specification.

**Related contracts:** `API-001` through `API-010`, `INT-001`, `CONF-006`.

**Expected evidence:** action matrix, OpenAPI operations and Module contract
links.

### BP-PC-027

The Blueprint specification **MUST** identify expected Domain Events and map
each event to its responsible producer and applicable cross-cutting
requirements.

**Responsible component:** Blueprint specification.

**Related contracts:** `EVT-001` through `EVT-010`, `INT-002`, `CONF-006`.

**Expected evidence:** event matrix, event schemas and producer contract tests.

### BP-PC-028

Every protected Blueprint action **MUST** identify its allowed actor, resource
scope, process or territory context and denial expectation.

**Responsible components:** composing Modules and authorization policies.

**Related contracts:** `AUTH-001`, `AUTH-002`, `AUTH-003`, `AUTH-006`,
`AUTH-007`.

**Expected evidence:** actor/action matrix and positive and negative policy
tests.

### BP-PC-029

Consultation publication, closure, moderation, institutional response, outcome
publication, policy changes and commitment state changes **MUST** produce
reviewable audit evidence.

**Responsible components:** Participation, Contribution, Accountability and
audit infrastructure.

**Related contracts:** `AUD-001` through `AUD-010`.

**Expected evidence:** auditable-action catalogue, audit schemas and state
transition tests.
