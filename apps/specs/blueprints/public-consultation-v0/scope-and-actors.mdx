## Purpose

Public Consultation v0 specifies a reusable civic process in which an
institution publishes a bounded public question, receives contributions,
publishes an outcome and exposes implementation progress.

The Blueprint covers this flow:

```text
Institution configures consultation
→ Institution publishes consultation
→ Public visitor discovers consultation
→ Participant checks eligibility
→ Participant submits contribution
→ Contribution is published according to moderation policy
→ Institution closes contribution period
→ Institution publishes outcome summary
→ Institution optionally responds to individual contributions
→ Institution creates commitments for announced actions
→ Institution updates progress and evidence
→ Public visitor follows implementation
```

## Actors

### Public Visitor

A Public Visitor discovers and reads published consultations, contributions,
institutional outcomes, commitments and public evidence. Reading public civic
content does not require an account.

### Participant

A Participant checks eligibility, creates and submits contributions, inspects
their contribution state and follows outcomes and commitments.

A Participant account is required to contribute. Additional identity,
residency or territorial verification is conditional on the consultation's
eligibility policy.

### Institution Manager

An Institution Manager configures, publishes, opens and closes consultations;
publishes outcome summaries; responds to contributions; and creates or updates
commitments within an authorized organization, process and territory scope.

### Moderator

Moderator is optional. When enabled, the role reviews or moderates
contributions according to the declared publication policy. It does not grant
institutional outcome or commitment permissions.

### Facilitator

Facilitator is optional. The role can support civic participation through
explicitly granted capabilities, but it does not inherit Moderator or
Institution Manager permissions.

## Identity And Eligibility Boundary

Hiaka keeps these concepts separate:

* Account authenticates access;
* Participant represents the civic actor within the process;
* Verified Identity holds protected verification data;
* Public Profile controls publicly visible participant information;
* Eligibility determines whether an action is permitted under process rules.

Verification must not automatically publish legal identity. Eligibility can
use verified attributes without exposing those attributes to the public.

## Non-Goals

Public Consultation v0 does not define:

* electoral voting or a legally binding ballot;
* advanced public deliberation;
* a formal decision or selection mechanism;
* a generic workflow engine;
* mandatory national identity verification;
* mandatory individual response to every contribution;
* one deployment topology or infrastructure vendor.

## Normative Requirements

### BP-PC-001

Published consultation information, published contributions, public outcome
summaries, commitments and public evidence **MUST** be readable without
authentication.

**Responsible components:** Participation, Contribution and Accountability.

**Related contracts:** `AUTH-002`, `AUTH-007`, `INT-001`.

**Expected evidence:** anonymous-access API tests and public journey tests.

### BP-PC-002

The Blueprint **MUST** support Public Visitor, Participant and Institution
Manager as required actors.

**Responsible component:** Blueprint composition.

**Related contracts:** `SPEC-007`, `AUTH-002`.

**Expected evidence:** actor/action matrix and end-to-end journey coverage.

### BP-PC-003

A person **MUST** have a Participant account before submitting a contribution.

**Responsible components:** Participation and authentication service.

**Related contracts:** `AUTH-001`, `AUTH-004`, `INT-003`.

**Expected evidence:** authenticated submission policy and use-case tests.

### BP-PC-004

Account, Participant, Verified Identity, Public Profile and Eligibility
**MUST** remain distinct concepts.

**Responsible components:** Participation, identity and verification services.

**Related contracts:** `AUTH-004`, `AUTH-005`, `EVT-009`.

**Expected evidence:** domain definitions, data-flow review and privacy tests.

### BP-PC-005

Moderator and Facilitator **MAY** be enabled as optional roles and **MUST NOT**
receive Institution Manager permissions implicitly.

**Responsible components:** Participation and authorization policy.

**Related contracts:** `AUTH-002`, `AUTH-007`, `AUTH-009`.

**Expected evidence:** role-policy definitions and negative authorization tests.
