## Normative Requirement

### BP-PC-030

A Public Consultation v0 conformance claim **MUST** provide evidence for every
applicable `BP-PC-*` requirement and disclose provisional Decide and Account
mapping gaps.

**Responsible component:** conforming product or implementation.

**Related contracts:** `CONF-001` through `CONF-010`.

**Expected evidence:** completed matrix, profile declaration, implementation
links and documented provisional-pattern gaps.

## Result Rules

Apply the [Conformance contract](/contracts/conformance):

* conformant requires evidence for every applicable mandatory requirement;
* partially conformant indicates incomplete evidence without a demonstrated
  mandatory violation;
* non-conformant indicates a demonstrated mandatory violation.

Decide and Account remain provisional Design System mappings. A report must
name these gaps and cannot represent them as complete pattern conformance.

## Risks And Trade-Offs

### Provisional Pattern Coverage

Institutional response and commitment tracking have clear Blueprint contracts,
but their Decide and Account experience mappings remain provisional.

### Machine-Readable Contract Depth

The Module v0 specs define semantic operations, payload minima and invariants.
OpenAPI and event-schema artifacts remain future work and must preserve the
Blueprint and Module ownership boundaries.

### Configurable Moderation

Supporting immediate and pre-moderated publication expands the conformance
surface but avoids prescribing one institutional policy.

### Optional Stages

Optional Deliberation and Evaluation preserve the full consultation model
without adding premature dependencies to the critical v0 path.

## Open Questions

* Decide pattern specification: resolve before complete response-pattern
  conformance is claimed.
* Account pattern specification: resolve before complete accountability-pattern
  conformance is claimed.
* Module payload versions: resolve during Participation, Contribution and
  Accountability v0 specs.
