Public Consultation v0 distinguishes the stages a civic actor experiences from
the operational state of the consultation.

## Civic Experience Stages

```text
DISCOVER
   ↓
CONTRIBUTE
   ↓
[DELIBERATE]
   ↓
[EVALUATE]
   ↓
RESPOND
   ↓
TRACK
```

### Discover

The public learns who owns the consultation, what question is open, who can
participate, which territory is concerned and when participation starts and
ends.

### Contribute

Eligible Participants submit contributions and can understand submission,
publication and moderation state.

### Deliberate

Optional. When enabled as a public discussion capability, this stage can add
the Deliberation Module through an additional contract.

### Evaluate

Optional. The institution can review contributions internally without a
Decision Module. A formal selection, prioritization or decision mechanism
requires an additional Decision contract.

### Respond

The institution publishes the consultation outcome, explains how contributions
were considered and can respond to individual contributions.

### Track

The public follows commitments, responsible actors, progress, evidence and
completion.

## Operational States

```text
DRAFT
  → PUBLISHED
  → OPEN
  → CLOSED
  → RESPONDED
  → TRACKING
  → COMPLETED
```

| State | Entry condition | Public meaning | Allowed next state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `DRAFT` | Institution creates the consultation | Not publicly available | `PUBLISHED` |
| `PUBLISHED` | Institution publishes validated consultation information | Publicly discoverable; contribution may not yet be open | `OPEN` |
| `OPEN` | Contribution period starts | Eligible Participants can submit | `CLOSED` |
| `CLOSED` | Contribution period ends or is closed by an authorized actor | New submissions are no longer accepted; outcome is pending | `RESPONDED` |
| `RESPONDED` | Public outcome summary is published | Institutional conclusion and next steps are visible | `TRACKING` or `COMPLETED` |
| `TRACKING` | At least one public commitment exists | Progress and evidence remain open to public review | `COMPLETED` |
| `COMPLETED` | Response obligations and required tracking have ended | Consultation lifecycle is complete | None |

`CLOSED` does not imply institutional response. A consultation without a
concrete announced action can move from `RESPONDED` to `COMPLETED` without
entering `TRACKING`.

## Ownership

Participation owns the consultation lifecycle and stage configuration. The
Blueprint constrains the sequence and public meaning but does not redefine
Participation business invariants.

Contribution and Accountability react to lifecycle transitions through their
published application and event contracts.

## Normative Requirements

### BP-PC-006

The Blueprint **MUST** distinguish civic experience stages from operational
process states.

**Responsible component:** Blueprint composition and Participation.

**Related contracts:** `SPEC-007`, `INT-002`.

**Expected evidence:** stage/state documentation and lifecycle tests.

### BP-PC-007

The operational lifecycle **MUST** support `DRAFT`, `PUBLISHED`, `OPEN`,
`CLOSED`, `RESPONDED`, `TRACKING` and `COMPLETED` as distinct states.

**Responsible component:** Participation.

**Related contracts:** `EVT-003`, `EVT-005`, `AUD-004`.

**Expected evidence:** state-machine or use-case tests and public status mapping.

### BP-PC-008

`CLOSED` **MUST** remain distinct from `RESPONDED`; closing contributions
**MUST NOT** imply that the institution has published its outcome.

**Responsible components:** Participation and Blueprint composition.

**Related contracts:** `EVT-001`, `AUD-004`.

**Expected evidence:** transition tests proving outcome publication is a
separate action.

### BP-PC-009

The `DELIBERATE` stage **MAY** be enabled and **MUST NOT** make the Deliberation
Module mandatory when it is absent.

**Responsible component:** Blueprint composition.

**Related contracts:** `INT-005`, `SPEC-007`.

**Expected evidence:** dependency manifest and lifecycle variants with and
without public deliberation.

### BP-PC-010

The `EVALUATE` stage **MAY** represent internal institutional review and
**MUST NOT** make the Decision Module mandatory unless a formal decision
mechanism is configured.

**Responsible component:** Blueprint composition.

**Related contracts:** `INT-005`, `SPEC-007`.

**Expected evidence:** configuration and journey tests showing internal review
without a Decision dependency.
