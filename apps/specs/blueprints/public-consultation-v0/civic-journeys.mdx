These journeys connect Public Consultation v0 actors, lifecycle, Modules and
Civic Design System patterns.

## Journey 1: Configure And Publish

**Actor:** Institution Manager.

**Preconditions:** authorized institution scope; consultation in `DRAFT`.

**Steps:**

1. Define title, question, purpose and responsible institution.
2. Define contribution period, territorial scope and eligibility policy.
3. Select `immediate` or `pre_moderated` publication policy.
4. Attach official documents where relevant.
5. Validate and publish the consultation.
6. Open the contribution period at the configured time or through an explicit
   authorized action.

**Outcome:** the consultation is publicly discoverable and clearly explains
who can participate, when and what happens after participation.

**Failure states:** missing required content, invalid dates, unauthorized
institution scope or unavailable required service.

**Components:** Participation; Inform and Consult patterns; conditional
territory and document services.

## Journey 2: Check Eligibility And Contribute

**Actor:** Participant.

**Preconditions:** consultation in `OPEN`; authenticated Participant account.

**Steps:**

1. Read the consultation and contribution policy.
2. Inspect eligibility requirements before entering contribution content.
3. Complete conditional verification when required.
4. Create and validate a contribution.
5. Submit the contribution.
6. Follow publication or moderation state.

**Outcome:** the contribution is published immediately after business
validation or awaits moderation under the declared policy.

**Failure states:** period closed, ineligible Participant, invalid content,
verification unavailable, moderation rejection or attachment failure.

**Components:** Participation and Contribution; Inform, Consult and Contribute
patterns; authentication plus conditional identity, territory and document
services.

## Journey 3: Close And Publish Outcome

**Actor:** Institution Manager.

**Preconditions:** consultation in `OPEN`; authorized process scope.

**Steps:**

1. Close the contribution period.
2. Review published contributions and any optional deliberation output.
3. Perform optional internal evaluation.
4. Publish the public outcome summary.
5. Optionally publish individual responses linked to contributions.
6. Identify concrete announced actions that require commitments.

**Outcome:** the consultation reaches `RESPONDED`; public readers can inspect
the institutional conclusion and next steps.

**Failure states:** unauthorized closure, incomplete outcome summary, private
review data exposed or announced action without a commitment.

**Components:** Participation and Contribution; Inform plus provisional Decide
mapping; audit infrastructure.

## Journey 4: Track Commitments

**Actors:** Institution Manager and Public Visitor.

**Preconditions:** a concrete action has been announced in the outcome or an
institutional response.

**Steps:**

1. Create a commitment linked to the consultation.
2. Identify responsible actor, initial status and expected progress path.
3. Add milestones where useful.
4. Publish progress updates and public evidence.
5. Complete the commitment with final evidence or explain why it ended without
   completion.
6. Let the public inspect progress history throughout tracking.

**Outcome:** the institution's announced action remains publicly traceable.

**Failure states:** missing responsible actor, progress without evidence where
evidence is expected, unauthorized update or broken consultation relation.

**Components:** Accountability; Inform plus provisional Account mapping;
conditional document service and required audit infrastructure.

## Contribution Publication Policies

### Immediate

After submission and business validation, the contribution is published. A
later moderation action can restrict or remove it under a documented policy and
must preserve audit evidence.

### Pre-Moderated

After submission and business validation, the contribution enters moderation.
It is published only after approval.

The selected policy is visible before the Participant starts contributing.
Moderation state and appropriate reason remain visible to the affected
Participant.

## Outcome Summary

The mandatory public outcome summary explains:

* what was received;
* how contributions were considered;
* what the institution concluded;
* which next steps are planned;
* which uncertainties or limitations remain.

Individual responses are optional. They remain visibly institutional and link
to the contribution they address.

## Commitments

When an outcome or individual response announces a concrete action, the
institution creates a commitment. The commitment exposes responsible actor,
status, progress history and public evidence.

## Normative Requirements

### BP-PC-016

Each consultation **MUST** declare either `immediate` or `pre_moderated` as its
contribution publication policy before contributions are submitted.

**Responsible components:** Participation and Contribution.

**Related contracts:** `INT-002`, `AUTH-002`.

**Expected evidence:** published configuration and journey tests for both
policies.

### BP-PC-017

A contribution **MUST NOT** be submitted when the consultation is outside its
open contribution period.

**Responsible components:** Participation and Contribution.

**Related contracts:** `AUTH-001`, `AUTH-002`, `AUD-004`.

**Expected evidence:** closed-period use-case and API tests.

### BP-PC-018

Applicable eligibility rules **MUST** be evaluated before a contribution is
accepted for publication.

**Responsible components:** Participation and conditional verification service.

**Related contracts:** `AUTH-003`, `AUTH-004`, `INT-003`.

**Expected evidence:** eligibility policy tests and verification adapter
contracts.

### BP-PC-019

Moderation status and an appropriate reason **MUST** be visible to the affected
Participant without exposing sensitive administrative information.

**Responsible component:** Contribution.

**Related contracts:** `AUTH-006`, `AUD-004`, `EVT-009`.

**Expected evidence:** participant-state journey, denial response and privacy
tests.

### BP-PC-020

Eligibility or identity verification **MUST NOT** expose verified legal
identity through the Participant's public profile by default.

**Responsible components:** Participation and identity or verification service.

**Related contracts:** `AUTH-004`, `AUTH-005`, `EVT-009`.

**Expected evidence:** data-flow review and public representation tests.

### BP-PC-021

The institution **MUST** publish one public outcome summary after the
contribution period closes.

**Responsible components:** Participation and Contribution.

**Related contracts:** `API-004`, `AUD-001`, `EVT-001`.

**Expected evidence:** outcome publication operation, event and lifecycle test.

### BP-PC-022

The outcome summary **MUST** explain what was received, how contributions were
considered, what was concluded, which next steps are planned and which
uncertainties remain.

**Responsible component:** Blueprint composition and institutional content.

**Related contracts:** `SPEC-007`, `AUD-005`.

**Expected evidence:** outcome schema and content conformance review.

### BP-PC-023

Individual institutional responses **MAY** be published and, when present,
**MUST** be visibly institutional and linked to the relevant contribution.

**Responsible component:** Contribution.

**Related contracts:** `AUTH-008`, `AUD-003`, `INT-002`.

**Expected evidence:** response relation schema and institutional attribution
tests.

### BP-PC-024

When an institution announces a concrete action, it **MUST** create a public
commitment for that action.

**Responsible component:** Accountability.

**Related contracts:** `API-004`, `AUD-001`, `EVT-001`.

**Expected evidence:** outcome-to-commitment journey and relation tests.

### BP-PC-025

A public commitment **MUST** identify its consultation, responsible actor,
current status, progress history and public evidence, and **SHOULD** link to
related contributions or the outcome summary where relevant.

**Responsible component:** Accountability.

**Related contracts:** `AUD-003`, `AUD-010`, `INT-002`.

**Expected evidence:** commitment representation, history and public evidence
tests.
