Public Consultation v0 is the first complete Blueprint specification used to
validate Hiaka's [Common Spec Structure](/framework/common-spec-structure),
component composition and evidence-based conformance model.

## Purpose

The Blueprint enables an institution to publish a bounded public question,
receive contributions, publish an accountable outcome and expose progress on
announced actions.

```text
Institution configures consultation
→ Institution publishes consultation
→ Public visitor discovers consultation
→ Participant checks eligibility
→ Participant submits contribution
→ Contribution is published according to moderation policy
→ Institution closes contribution period
→ Institution publishes outcome summary
→ Institution optionally responds to individual contributions
→ Institution creates commitments for announced actions
→ Institution updates progress and evidence
→ Public visitor follows implementation
```

## Composition Summary

Required Modules:

* Participation
* Contribution
* Accountability

Optional Modules:

* Deliberation, when public deliberation is enabled;
* Decision, when a formal decision mechanism is configured.

Required Civic Design System patterns are Inform, Consult and Contribute.
Decide and Account are provisional mappings until their dedicated pattern specs
exist.

## Assumptions And Dependencies

The v0 flow assumes public read access, authenticated contribution, a declared
eligibility policy and an explicit contribution publication policy. Shared
Digital Services are consumed through published interfaces and classified as
required, conditional or optional.

## Boundaries

The Blueprint composes Modules and patterns. It does not own Module DTOs,
persistence models or internal business invariants. It does not require
electoral voting, advanced public deliberation, formal decision mechanisms or
a generic workflow engine.

## Risks And Open Questions

Decide and Account conformance remains provisional. Detailed Module payloads
and invariants are deferred to Participation, Contribution and Accountability
v0 specs.
