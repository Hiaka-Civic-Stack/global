description: "Deferred Module boundary for configurable civic decision capabilities."
---

<Note>**Status: draft overview.** This is not an implementation-ready Module specification.</Note>

Decision will own configurable support, prioritization, selection and decision
records. It must not assume that every civic decision is an election or vote.

The Module is optional for Public Consultation v0 and is outside the Sprint 5
critical path. Internal institutional evaluation does not require this Module.
No Decision conformance claim exists until a dedicated v0 specification defines
its requirements and evidence.
