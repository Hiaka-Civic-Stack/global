---
title: "Deliberation Overview"
description: "Deferred Module boundary for public discussion and argumentation capabilities."
---

<Note>**Status: draft overview.** This is not an implementation-ready Module specification.</Note>

Deliberation will own reusable public discussion, comments, replies,
argumentation and discussion moderation. It will not own contribution
submission, formal decisions or process lifecycle.

The Module is optional for Public Consultation v0 and is outside the Sprint 5
critical path. No Deliberation conformance claim exists until a dedicated v0
specification defines its requirements and evidence.
