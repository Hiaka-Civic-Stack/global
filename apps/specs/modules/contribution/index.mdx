Contribution owns participant-authored submissions and their publication,
moderation and individual institutional-response lifecycle.

## Responsibilities

* drafts, validation and submission;
* immediate or pre-moderated publication;
* withdrawal and moderation state with appropriate reasons;
* attachment and geographic references;
* individual institutional responses;
* references from contributions to process outcomes.

## Non-Responsibilities

Contribution does not own civic process lifecycle, eligibility policies,
verified identity, global process outcomes, deliberation, formal decisions,
commitments or binary document storage.

## Dependencies

It consumes published Participation contracts for process state, participation
conditions and eligibility decisions. These are interface dependencies, not a
database or Blueprint dependency.

## Actors And Consumers

Participants own drafts and submit contributions. Public visitors read
published contributions. Institution managers publish individual responses,
and moderators act only when a declared policy grants that role. Products and
other Modules consume published APIs and events.

## Assumptions, Risks And Open Questions

The specification assumes process conditions can be resolved through a
versioned contract. Provider unavailability must fail safely rather than permit
an unverified submission. No unresolved question prevents the v0 contract;
content-policy details remain deployment configuration.
