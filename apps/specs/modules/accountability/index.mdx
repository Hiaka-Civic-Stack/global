Accountability turns announced institutional actions into public commitments
whose responsibility, progress, evidence and history can be inspected.

## Responsibilities

* public commitments and source references;
* responsible organizations;
* milestones and progress updates;
* public evidence references;
* completion or explained closure;
* attributable status and progress history.

## Non-Responsibilities

Accountability does not decide what an institution should do, own source
processes, outcomes or contributions, implement document storage, or redefine
institutional identity and authorization.

## Dependencies

Source objects are referenced through stable published identifiers. Document,
organization, notification and audit integrations use ports. No Blueprint,
database or provider is mandatory.

## Actors And Consumers

Public visitors inspect commitments and history. Institution and commitment
managers create and update commitments within authorized scope. Products and
other Modules consume public commitment APIs and events.

## Assumptions, Risks And Open Questions

The specification assumes source and organization references can be validated
through versioned contracts. Long-lived public history increases retention and
correction obligations, so implementations must preserve provenance without
silently rewriting records. No unresolved question prevents the v0 contract.
