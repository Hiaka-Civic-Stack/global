This mapping proves the Civic Design System against the first Hiaka blueprint.

It does not define the blueprint lifecycle or module business rules. It shows
how civic intents, semantic concepts and patterns guide the experience of
Public Consultation v0.

The Blueprint specification owns:

* [scope and actors](/blueprints/public-consultation-v0/scope-and-actors);
* [lifecycle](/blueprints/public-consultation-v0/lifecycle);
* [component composition](/blueprints/public-consultation-v0/composition);
* [civic journeys](/blueprints/public-consultation-v0/civic-journeys).

This mapping remains the Design System experience view. It must not become a
second source of truth for lifecycle or composition.

## Mapping Table

| Blueprint moment | Civic intent | Semantic concepts | Pattern | Module touchpoints | Conformance checks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Institution creates consultation | Understand a public process | institution, process, stage, territorial scope | Inform | Participation | Process has responsible actor, draft state, scope and next step. |
| Institution publishes consultation | Understand a public process | process, status, stage, official document, participation period | Inform, Consult | Participation, documents | Published state, dates, topic and official documents are visible. |
| Participant checks participation | Know whether one can participate | participant, eligibility, verification, territory, action availability | Inform, Consult | Participation, identity or verification service | Eligibility and action availability are explained before contribution. |

| Contribution is published | Contribute to a bounded civic process | published contribution, moderation state, participant, evidence | Contribute | Contribution | Published versus moderated state is visible and participant content is distinct. |

## Intent Coverage

Public Consultation v0 covers these Civic Design System intents:

* [understand a public process](/design-system/intents/understand-process);
* [know whether one can participate](/design-system/intents/know-participation-eligibility);
* [contribute to a bounded civic process](/design-system/intents/contribute-bounded-process);
* verify an official response, decision or rationale;
* follow a decision, commitment or implementation progress.

The final two intents are mapped here but not yet expanded into dedicated
Sprint 1A specs.

## Pattern Coverage

Public Consultation v0 uses these Civic Design System patterns:

* [Inform](/design-system/patterns/inform);
* [Consult](/design-system/patterns/consult);
* [Contribute](/design-system/patterns/contribute);
* Decide;
* Account.

Decide and Account are included because the blueprint includes institutional
response and commitment tracking. They are not expanded into dedicated Sprint
1A pattern specs and remain provisional mappings in the Blueprint's
[conformance report](/blueprints/public-consultation-v0/conformance).

## Boundary Notes

This mapping does not:

* define Participation, Contribution or Accountability business rules;
* define API endpoints or event names;
* define visual tokens, UI components or Figma artifacts;
* replace the Public Consultation v0 blueprint spec.

It is the Design System view of the [Public Consultation v0
Blueprint](/blueprints/public-consultation-v0).
