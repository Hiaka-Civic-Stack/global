title: "Pattern: Consult"

---

## Purpose

Consult frames a bounded public participation request so people understand who
is being asked, what input is expected, when participation is open and how
responses will be handled.

## Civic Intent

Primary intents:

* [understand a public process](/design-system/intents/understand-process);
* [know whether one can participate](/design-system/intents/know-participation-eligibility).

Related intent:

* [contribute to a bounded civic process](/design-system/intents/contribute-bounded-process).

## Semantic Concepts

Required concepts:

* consultation;
* target public;
* institution;
* stage;
* participation period;
* eligibility;
* topic;
* question;
* response expectation.

## Patterns

Consult usually composes Inform and Contribute.

It establishes the civic frame for participation before people submit input.

## Module Touchpoints

Typical module touchpoints:

* Participation for consultation process setup, stages, target public and
  eligibility policy references;
* Contribution for accepted contribution types and response expectations.

## Accessibility

Consultation questions and participation periods should be readable before
starting a contribution flow.

The product should not require users to open a modal or hidden control to
understand what is being requested.

## Localization

Consultation topics and questions must support long Malagasy and French text.

The pattern should allow local civic vocabulary without changing the underlying
semantic meaning.

## Conformance Checks

* Is the target public clear?
* Is the consultation topic or question clear?
* Is the participation period visible?
* Is the expected input type stated?
* Is the response expectation explained?
* Is eligibility explained before contribution?

## Examples

In Public Consultation v0, Consult frames the published consultation and
explains what kind of contribution the institution is asking for.

## Non-Goals

Consult does not define contribution persistence, moderation policy,
authorization rules, visual tokens or UI components.
