## Purpose

Inform helps people understand official civic information, process rules,
dates, territorial scope, responsible actors and next steps.

## Civic Intent

Primary intents:

* [understand a public process](/design-system/intents/understand-process);
* [know whether one can participate](/design-system/intents/know-participation-eligibility).

## Semantic Concepts

Required concepts:

* institution;
* process;
* stage;
* status;
* territorial scope;
* official document;
* participation period;
* eligibility;
* next step.

## Patterns

Inform can stand alone for public orientation or support Consult, Contribute,
Decide and Account.

It should not hide process uncertainty behind marketing copy.

## Module Touchpoints

Typical module touchpoints:

* Participation for process metadata, lifecycle state, stages and eligibility
  policy references;
* Shared document service for official documents;
* Territory service for territorial scope.

## Accessibility

Key status and next-step information should be represented as text, not color
alone.

Documents should have descriptive labels and accessible file metadata where
possible.

## Localization

Official information should support Malagasy and French, including longer
labels and local civic vocabulary.

Territorial labels should come from configuration or data, not hard-coded UI
text.

## Conformance Checks

* Is the responsible institution visible?
* Is the process status visible?
* Are dates, scope and next steps visible?
* Are official documents distinguishable from participant content?
* Does the product expose enough context before asking for participation?

## Examples

In Public Consultation v0, Inform appears on the consultation overview before
any contribution action.

## Non-Goals

Inform does not define document storage, process lifecycle rules, visual
tokens, UI components or legal publication policy.
