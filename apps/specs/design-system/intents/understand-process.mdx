---
title: "Intent: Understand A Public Process"
description: "Spec for helping people understand the state, rules and scope of a civic process."
---

## Purpose

People should be able to understand what a public process is, who runs it,
what stage it is in, who can participate and what happens next.

## Civic Intent

Primary intent:

* understand a public process.

Related intents:

* know whether one can participate;
* verify an official response, decision or rationale;
* follow a decision, commitment or implementation progress.

## Semantic Concepts

Required concepts:

* process;
* institution;
* stage;
* status;
* territorial scope;
* participation period;
* eligibility;
* official documents;
* next step.

## Patterns

Primary pattern:

* [Inform](/design-system/patterns/inform).

Related patterns:

* [Consult](/design-system/patterns/consult);
* [Contribute](/design-system/patterns/contribute);
* Decide;
* Account.

## Module Touchpoints

Typical module touchpoints:

* Participation for process state, stages, participation periods and
  eligibility policy references;
* Contribution when the process exposes participant submissions;
* Accountability when the process includes commitments or progress tracking.

The intent does not define these module rules.

## Accessibility

The product should make process status, dates and next steps reachable by
keyboard and understandable without relying only on color.

Status labels should be readable by assistive technology and remain clear when
content wraps on small screens.

## Localization

Process names, stage labels, territorial names and next-step text must support
Malagasy and French without assuming short labels.

Date and territory formatting should be localizable.

## Conformance Checks

* Can a first-time visitor identify the institution responsible for the
  process?
* Is the current process stage visible?
* Are participation dates or deadlines visible where relevant?
* Is territorial scope explained?
* Is the next expected public step stated?
* Are official documents or evidence discoverable when present?

## Examples

In Public Consultation v0, a participant should understand that a consultation
has been published, what topic it covers, which territory it applies to and
whether contribution is currently open.

## Non-Goals

This intent does not define process lifecycle rules, API fields, persistence,
visual tokens or UI components.
