## Purpose

People should be able to contribute input, evidence or proposals while
understanding the process boundary, publication behavior, moderation state and
expected institutional follow-up.

## Civic Intent

Primary intent:

* contribute to a bounded civic process.

Related intents:

* understand a public process;
* know whether one can participate;
* verify an official response, decision or rationale.

## Semantic Concepts

Required concepts:

* participant;
* contribution;
* draft;
* published contribution;
* attachment;
* moderation state;
* eligibility;
* stage;
* official response;
* evidence.

## Patterns

Primary pattern:

* [Contribute](/design-system/patterns/contribute).

Related patterns:

* [Inform](/design-system/patterns/inform);
* [Consult](/design-system/patterns/consult);
* Deliberate;
* Decide.

## Module Touchpoints

Typical module touchpoints:

* Participation for process state, stage and eligibility;
* Contribution for draft, submission, publication, moderation, attachment and
  response state;
* Accountability if the contribution later leads to a commitment.

## Accessibility

Forms should expose required fields, validation errors, draft state and
publication confirmation in accessible text.

Users should not lose entered content when recovering from validation or
eligibility errors.

## Localization

Field labels, helper text, moderation labels and publication confirmation must
support Malagasy and French.

Long contribution titles and attachment names should wrap without obscuring
actions.

## Conformance Checks

* Does the product explain what the contribution is for?
* Is draft versus published state clear?
* Are required fields and validation errors accessible?
* Is moderation or publication status visible?
* Does the participant understand what happens after submission?
* Are attachments and evidence represented without implying they are official
  responses?

## Examples

In Public Consultation v0, a participant submits a contribution during the
open stage, sees publication status, and can later distinguish their
contribution from the institution's response.

## Non-Goals

This intent does not define contribution storage, moderation policy, API shape,
visual tokens or UI components.
