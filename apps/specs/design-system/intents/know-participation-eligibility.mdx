## Purpose

People should understand whether they can participate before they invest effort
in a civic action.

The product should explain eligibility, verification, territorial scope,
deadlines and submission consequences before requiring the user to act.

## Civic Intent

Primary intent:

* know whether one can participate.

Related intents:

* understand a public process;
* contribute to a bounded civic process.

## Semantic Concepts

Required concepts:

* participant;
* eligibility;
* verified identity;
* public profile;
* territorial scope;
* participation period;
* stage;
* action availability;
* submission consequence.

## Patterns

Primary patterns:

* [Inform](/design-system/patterns/inform);
* [Consult](/design-system/patterns/consult).

Related pattern:

* [Contribute](/design-system/patterns/contribute).

## Module Touchpoints

Typical module touchpoints:

* Participation for eligibility policy references, active stage and
  participation period;
* Shared identity or participant verification services for verification state;
* Contribution when eligibility affects submission behavior.

The intent does not expose legal identity publicly and does not define
authorization rules by itself.

## Accessibility

Eligibility information should be available before form submission and should
not depend on hidden hover-only content.

Disabled or unavailable actions should explain the reason and recovery path.

## Localization

Eligibility explanations must tolerate long civic vocabulary in Malagasy and
French.

Territorial names and verification labels should be localizable and not
hard-coded to one administrative model.

## Conformance Checks

* Is eligibility explained before action?
* Is verification requirement explained separately from public identity?
* Is territorial scope visible?
* Is the participation period or stage constraint visible?
* If action is unavailable, does the interface explain why?
* Does the product avoid exposing legal identity as the public participation
  identity?

## Examples

In Public Consultation v0, a participant should know whether contributions are
open, whether they need verification, and whether the consultation territory
applies to them before submitting.

## Non-Goals

This intent does not define identity provider behavior, authorization policy,
module persistence, visual tokens or UI components.
