The Pattern Layer realizes civic intents through the Semantic Layer.

Civic Patterns are not a standalone top-level layer of Hiaka. They live inside
the Civic Design System as reusable civic experience patterns.

## Initial Pattern Catalog

## Inform

Publish official information, process rules, dates, territorial scope and
institutional responsibilities.

Common surfaces:

* process overview;
* official document list;
* timeline;
* eligibility explanation;
* public status banner.

## Consult

Ask a public, community or stakeholder group for input within a bounded civic
process.

Common surfaces:

* consultation brief;
* participation period;
* question or topic list;
* target public;
* response expectation.

## Contribute

Allow participants to submit ideas, proposals, documents, comments or evidence.

Common surfaces:

* contribution form;
* draft state;
* publication confirmation;
* attachment list;
* moderation status.

## Deliberate

Support discussion, comparison, argumentation, moderation and collective
sense-making.

Common surfaces:

* discussion thread;
* argument list;
* moderation notice;
* reply composer;
* comparison view.

## Decide

Represent public decisions, institutional responses, mandates, rationales and
outcomes.

Common surfaces:

* decision record;
* rationale;
* selected option;
* official response;
* publication date.

## Account

Track commitments, milestones, evidence, progress and public follow-up.

Common surfaces:

* commitment list;
* milestone timeline;
* progress update;
* evidence attachment;
* responsible actor summary.

## Pattern Review

When adding or adapting a pattern, review:

* civic intent;
* actor and authority;
* participant expectation;
* required semantic concepts;
* required evidence;
* accessibility behavior;
* localization behavior;
* relationship to Civic Stack Model concepts.

Pattern specs follow the [Design System Spec Structure](/design-system/spec-structure).
