---
title: "Standards And Conformance"
description: "How products are reviewed against the Civic Design System model."
---

Standards and conformance make the Civic Design System reviewable.

They are part of the Operational Model. They are not a fourth conceptual layer.

```text
Operational Model
  -> Standards and conformance
  -> Reference implementation artifacts
```

## Review Scope

Civic Design System conformance checks whether a product:

* starts from explicit civic intents;
* uses the shared semantic language consistently;
* applies the correct Civic Design System patterns;
* makes process state, actor responsibility, eligibility and next steps clear;
* preserves accessibility and localization under realistic content;
* exposes public evidence and accountability where relevant;
* respects the boundaries of the Civic Stack Model.

## Civic Clarity

* Is the process status visible?
* Is the institutional actor clear?
* Is the participant action understandable?
* Is the next step explained?
* Are dates, stages and deadlines visible where relevant?

## Public Trust

* Does the interface explain who decides?
* Does it distinguish participant content from institutional response?
* Does it expose rationale or evidence for public decisions?
* Does it avoid hiding uncertainty behind polished language?

## Inclusion And Accessibility

* Does the flow work on small screens?
* Does it tolerate long Malagasy and French text?
* Does it remain readable under zoom?
* Does it avoid assuming high bandwidth?
* Does it explain eligibility before requiring action?
* Can key actions be reached by keyboard?
* Are statuses, errors and next steps labeled clearly?

## Auditability

* Are important public actions traceable?
* Are actor, time, state and reason visible where needed?
* Can users inspect public evidence or progress history?
* Does the product separate auditability from decorative timelines?

## Outcome

An early conformance review can classify findings as:

* aligned;
* partially aligned;
* needs design clarification;
* needs product-domain clarification;
* needs implementation contract clarification.

The last two categories stay separate. Product-domain questions belong to the
Civic Stack Model. Specification and conformance questions belong to Civic
Stack Specs.
