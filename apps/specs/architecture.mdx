---
title: "Architecture"
description: "The core architectural model behind Hiaka."
---

Hiaka separates civic process design from reusable civic capabilities.

## Invariant

```text
Civic Blueprint
      ↓
composes

Civic Patterns
      ↓
coordinate

Civic Modules
      ↓
consume

Shared Digital Services
```

Blueprints should not become monolithic applications. Patterns should not own
core business data. Modules should not depend on a single blueprint.
