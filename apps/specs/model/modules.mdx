Civic Modules are the reusable capability components of the Civic Stack Model.

They answer:

**What civic capability does the stack provide?**

## Responsibilities

A Civic Module owns a coherent civic domain boundary, including its concepts,
business rules, use cases, permissions and state transitions.

The initial Modules are:

* Participation;
* Contribution;
* Deliberation;
* Decision;
* Accountability.

## Boundaries

A Module must not depend on one Civic Blueprint. The Participation Module, for
example, must remain usable by Public Consultation, Citizen Initiative and
Participatory Budget Blueprints.

A Module does not define how every interaction is presented to a civic actor.
That experience language belongs to the Civic Design System. Generic identity,
notification, storage and territory capabilities belong to Shared Digital
Services.

## Relationship To Blueprints

Civic Blueprints select and coordinate Modules to support a complete civic
process. A Blueprint may constrain which Module capabilities it requires, but
it does not absorb their business rules.

## Model Versus Specs

This page defines the conceptual role of Civic Modules. Read the [Civic Modules
specs overview](/modules/overview) for the specification entry point. Detailed
Module contracts will define APIs, events, authorization and conformance.
