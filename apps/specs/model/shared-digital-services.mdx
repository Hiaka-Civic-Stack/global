---
title: "Shared Digital Services"
description: "Generic digital capabilities that support the Civic Stack Model through explicit interfaces."
---

Shared Digital Services provide generic infrastructure used by Civic Modules
and Civic Blueprints.

They answer:

**Which non-civic capabilities does the stack need to consume?**

Examples include:

* authentication and identity;
* participant verification;
* territory registries;
* notifications;
* document storage;
* search and GIS;
* audit infrastructure.

## Supporting Boundary

Shared Digital Services are not a fourth civic component layer. They support
the model without owning civic participation rules, experience patterns or
complete process definitions.

## Integration Rule

Civic components access Shared Digital Services through explicit ports and
adapters. Core domain logic must not depend directly on a particular identity
provider, object store, notification vendor or GIS product.

```text
Civic component
      |
      +-- Identity port
      +-- Territory port
      +-- Document port
      +-- Notification port
```

## Model Versus Specs

This page defines the supporting role of Shared Digital Services. Future Civic
Stack Specs will define the shared interfaces, data exchange expectations and
interoperability requirements needed by implementations.
